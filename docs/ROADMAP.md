# KrushiMitra AI — Roadmap, Revenue & Competitive Moat

## Roadmap
### 30 days — "Prove the wedge with 10 farmers"
Stand up a minimal FastAPI (auth, `/copilot/ask` with Bhashini STT/TTS + Claude+RAG, `/vision/diagnose`
via vision-LLM, store images). Skip trained models. Point the Expo app's services at it. Get it onto
10 of the father's contacts' phones. Watch them use it.

### 90 days — "MVP + data flywheel on"
3-crop fine-tuned detectors live · severity v1 (LLM-rubric, "beta") · crop-twin timeline + health score ·
feedback loop collecting labels · 100 farmers · first 1–2k labeled images.

### 6 months — "The moat shows up"
Segmentation-based severity v2 trained on own data · progression reports · 1,000 farmers · first FPO
conversation · apply to agri-incubator/grant (a-IDEA/ICAR, NABARD, accelerators).

### 1 year — "Defensible product + first revenue"
5–10k farmers · premium tier · B2B pilot (FPO / input-dealer dashboard) · 20k+ labeled images with
expert-verified severity gold sets · raise small pre-seed or win grants on the dataset story.

### 3 years — "Maharashtra's agri copilot"
5 crops · 3 languages · 100k+ farmers · yield + weather + market intelligence · dataset as a licensable
asset · marketplace/insurance partnerships · KrushiGPT as the unified copilot.

## Revenue model (sequenced — not all at once)
- **Now / free:** everything free — buying data and trust, not revenue.
- **Freemium (yr 1):** free = basic diagnosis + copilot; **Premium ~₹99–199/season/crop** = unlimited
  severity tracking, progression reports, priority agronomist escalation, weather+irrigation.
- **B2B (yr 1–2, the real money):** sell dashboard + API to FPOs, input dealers, agri-input companies
  wanting outbreak intelligence + farmer reach. B2B ARPU ≫ smallholder B2C in India.
- **Later:** input-sales commission, insurance/credit partnerships (crop-health data → underwriting),
  advisory services, anonymized dataset/insight licensing.

> Reality: B2C subscriptions from smallholders are hard — treat them as an engagement signal.
> FPO/B2B + data licensing is where a solo founder actually monetizes.

## Competitive moat
| Competitor | Their strength | Where KrushiMitra wins |
|---|---|---|
| **Plantix** | Best global detection | Generic; no severity, no voice, no local trust, no Marathi copilot |
| **DeHaat / AgroStar** | Input distribution, scale | Commerce-first, not AI-agronomist; deeper decision intelligence per crop |
| **Cropin / Fasal** | Enterprise / IoT | B2B/sensor-heavy; they don't own the smallholder voice layer |

**Five compounding moats:**
1. **Voice-first Marathi/Hindi copilot** — localization depth the giants won't match.
2. **Severity + progression intelligence** — the decision layer above commoditized detection.
3. **Digital Crop Twin** — longitudinal data that improves with every photo; regional outbreak signal.
4. **Regional × crop depth** (Maharashtra × 3 crops) — narrow-but-deep beats broad-but-shallow.
5. **Proprietary field + severity dataset** via the father's channel — hardest to copy, compounds fastest.

**Risks to respect:** advice liability (cite sources, hedge dosages) · Marathi dialect STT/TTS quality ·
trust takes months · can't out-engineer funded teams → win the wedge, own the data.
